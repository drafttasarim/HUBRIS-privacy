Write a support email

HUBRIS is in development and has no released version yet. Send development or privacy questions to this address.

What to include

  • A short subject and a description of your question.
  • For a technical issue, your device model, operating system and game version, if applicable.
  • Steps to reproduce the issue and screenshots with personal information hidden.

Do not send passwords, verification codes, identity numbers or payment details.

Common questions

Where can I download the game?

The game has not been released on the App Store. No confirmed release date has been announced.

Which platforms are planned?

Development is planned for iPhone/iPad and Mac.

Will progress sync between devices?

Local on-device saves are planned for the first release. Automatic cross-platform saves have not been implemented.

How can I make a privacy request?

Email info@drafttasarim.com with the subject “HUBRIS Privacy”. The privacy page explains the current status.
